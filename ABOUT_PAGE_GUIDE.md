### 📁 **Photos to Add to `public/images/about/`:**

#### **1. Artist Portrait**
- **File name**: `fabian-portrait.jpg` (or .png)
- **What**: Professional photo of you
- **Size**: At least 800x600px
- **Style**: Professional, artistic, or casual - whatever represents you best

#### **2. Studio Photos**
- **File names**: `studio-1.jpg`, `studio-2.jpg`, `studio-3.jpg`
- **What**: Your workspace, studio, or where you create art
- **Ideas**: 
  - You working on a painting
  - Your art supplies and tools

  - Artworks in progress

#### **3. Process Photos**
- **File names**: `process-1.jpg`, `process-2.jpg`
- **What**: You creating your kinetic art
- **Ideas**:
  - Working with acrylic sheets
  - Installing LED lighting
  - Painting process
  - Testing optical effects

#### **4. Gallery/Exhibition Photos**
- **File names**: `exhibition-1.jpg`, `exhibition-2.jpg`
- **What**: Your art in galleries or exhibitions
- **Ideas**:
  - Artworks displayed in galleries
  - You at exhibitions
  - Collectors viewing your work
  - Art in different lighting

#### **5. Behind-the-Scenes Photos**
- **File names**: `behind-scenes-1.jpg`, `behind-scenes-2.jpg`
- **What**: Any other relevant photos
- **Ideas**:

  - Art in different settings
  - Close-up details of your work
