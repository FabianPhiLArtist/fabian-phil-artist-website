# 🧭 Navigation Visibility Fix - Menu Always Readable

## ✅ **Navigation Menu Visibility Issues Fixed!**

### 🎯 **Problem Solved:**
The navigation menu was becoming too dark/blurred and hard to read when navigating to pages other than the Gallery.

---

## **🔧 What I Fixed:**

### **1. Consistent Background:**
- ✅ **Before**: Navigation changed from transparent to white/95 based on scroll
- ✅ **After**: Navigation always has `bg-white/95 backdrop-blur-md shadow-lg`
- ✅ **Result**: Consistent, visible background on all pages

### **2. Improved Text Colors:**
- ✅ **Navigation Links**: Changed from `text-gray-700` to `text-gray-900` (darker, more readable)
- ✅ **Hover States**: Changed to `hover:text-blue-600` for better contrast
- ✅ **Logo**: Kept `text-gray-900` (already good)
- ✅ **Instagram Links**: Changed from `text-gray-700` to `text-gray-900`

### **3. Enhanced Visual Definition:**
- ✅ **Added Border**: `border-b border-gray-200/50` for subtle separation
- ✅ **Maintained Shadow**: `shadow-lg` for depth
- ✅ **Backdrop Blur**: `backdrop-blur-md` for modern glass effect

### **4. Mobile Menu Improvements:**
- ✅ **Mobile Links**: Same color improvements as desktop
- ✅ **Mobile Button**: Better visibility with `text-gray-900`
- ✅ **Mobile Instagram**: Consistent with desktop styling
