## ADDITIONAL INFORMATION

### Medium Specifications
- **Acrylic Sheets:** All works are created on two acrylic sheets for depth and optical effects
- **LED Integration:** Selected works feature LED lighting elements
- **Fluorescent Elements:** Pop glasses collection includes UV-reactive materials
- **Digital Fusion:** Some works combine traditional painting with digital printing

### Exhibition Considerations
- Works require proper lighting to showcase optical effects
- LED-equipped pieces need electrical access
- Fluorescent elements benefit from UV lighting in dark environments
- All works are ready to hang with appropriate hardware

### Shipping & Handling
- Professional crating required for international transport
- Insurance coverage recommended
- Works are fragile due to acrylic sheet construction
- Special handling instructions provided with each piece
